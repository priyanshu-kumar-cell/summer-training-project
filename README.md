# Scroll, Sleep & Stress: Social Media's Impact on Student Life 📱💤

An interactive Power BI dashboard exploring how social media usage affects sleep patterns and mental health among students. Built on a Kaggle dataset of 1,705 student survey responses across 111 countries, the project turns raw behavioural data into a clear, visual story.

## 📁 Dataset
Source: [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/) — `Social_Media_Impact_on_Life.csv` (1,705 rows, 11 columns), covering age, gender, academic level, country, daily usage hours, most-used platform, sleep hours, mental health score, academic impact, and overall impact rating.

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights
- **KPI Cards:** Avg Daily Usage (5.10 hrs), Avg Sleep (6.60 hrs)
- **Daily Usage Distribution** (bar chart) — spread of usage hours across students
- **Most Preferred Platform** (donut chart) — Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook lead
- **Gender vs Mental Health Score** (column chart)
- **Age vs Mental Health Trend** (line chart)
- **Usage vs Sleep Duration** (scatter plot)
- **Slicers:** Gender and Platform tables for interactive filtering

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Strong negative correlation between usage and sleep (**−0.82**)
- Strong negative correlation between usage and mental health (**−0.83**)
- Strong positive correlation between sleep and mental health (**+0.79**)
- ~59% of students feel social media affects their academic performance

## 🛠️ Tool Used
Microsoft Power BI Desktop — data modeling, DAX measures, and visualization.

## 📌 Conclusion
The dashboard shows that higher social media usage is consistently linked to less sleep and lower mental health scores, replacing assumptions with real, data-backed evidence.
